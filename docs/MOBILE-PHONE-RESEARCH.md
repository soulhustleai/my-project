## OPTION B: GET YOUR PHONE BILL TO $0 OR NEAR-$0

### Tier 1: Completely FREE ($0/month)

#### 1. TextNow Free Flex Plan — $0/month
- **What you get:** Unlimited talk & text + 1GB essential data (email, maps, finance, rideshare)
- **Network:** T-Mobile (new activations on AT&T as of Sept 2025)
- **Catch:** Ad-supported. No high-speed browsing data. Need to buy $4.99 SIM once.
- **Data add-ons:** $0.99/hour to $35.99/month for full data
- **Rating:** 4.8/5 App Store (884K reviews), 4.6/5 Google Play (1.59M reviews)
- **Best for:** Basic talk/text needs. If you're on WiFi most of the time, this is the move.
- **Website:** textnow.com

#### 2. Helium Mobile Zero Plan — $0/month
- **What you get:** 100 minutes, 300 texts, 3GB data
- **Network:** T-Mobile + Helium decentralized network
- **Catch:** Limited minutes/texts. Privacy concerns (location tracking). Newer service.
- **Website:** hellohelium.com

#### 3. Google Voice (WiFi Only) — $0/month
- **What you get:** Free phone number, unlimited US calls & texts over WiFi
- **Network:** WiFi only (no cellular)
- **Catch:** Need WiFi everywhere. Need a phone number to sign up initially. No 911 reliability.
- **Setup:** Download Google Voice app → Sign in → Settings → "Prefer Wi-Fi and mobile data"
- **Best for:** If you're always near WiFi (home, office, coffee shops)

#### 4. Federal Lifeline Program — $0/month
- **What you get:** Free phone + free service
- **Catch:** Income must be at/below 135% Federal Poverty Level (~$21,546/yr single, ~$44,506/yr family of 4)
- **Note:** ACP ended June 2024. Lifeline is the only remaining federal program.

### The Play: Recommended Strategy

**Immediate move (save 100%):**
1. Port your number to Google Voice ($20 one-time fee)
2. Get TextNow SIM ($4.99) for cellular backup
3. Use Google Voice as primary over WiFi
4. Use TextNow when you need cellular

2. Use WiFi calling everywhere possible
3. **Saves ~$50-130/mo vs Optimum/major carriers**

## OPTION A: START YOUR OWN MVNO (Like Mint Mobile)

### What Is an MVNO?
A Mobile Virtual Network Operator leases network access from T-Mobile/AT&T/Verizon at wholesale rates, rebrands it, and sells to consumers. You don't build towers — you rent capacity.

### Business Models (Lightest → Heaviest)

#### 1. Branded Reseller (Easiest)
- Marketing-only entity. MNO handles everything technical.
- You just sell under your brand.
- **Startup cost:** $50K–$150K
- **Time to launch:** 1–3 months
- **Control:** Minimal (pricing, brand only)

#### 2. Light MVNO (Middle Ground)
- You control billing, pricing, customer service
- MNO provides network + some infrastructure
- **Startup cost:** $150K–$500K
- **Time to launch:** 3–6 months
- **Control:** Moderate

#### 3. Full MVNO (Maximum Control)
- You own core infrastructure (HLR, MSC)
- Only lease the radio access network
- **Startup cost:** $500K–$1M+
- **Time to launch:** 6–12 months
- **Control:** Full

### Modern "MVNO in a Box" Platforms (Game Changers)

These platforms let you launch an MVNO without building anything from scratch:

#### Gigs.com — "Stripe for Phone Plans"
- **What:** API-first telecom platform. One integration, multiple carriers.
- **Launch time:** 3–6 weeks (or days with no-code tools)
- **Cost:** ~$3–$8/subscriber/month + $5K–$25K implementation
- **Features:** White-label apps, billing, eSIM, AI customer support
- **Customers:** Revolut, Klarna, Nubank, Walmart
- **Funding:** $93M raised (YC, Google Gradient, Ribbit Capital)
- **Best for:** Tech-forward MVNO launch

#### Spenza — "Stripe + Shopify for Connectivity"
- **What:** Turnkey MVNO platform
- **Launch time:** As fast as 7 days
- **Features:** No-code tools, pre-integrated billing, white-label apps, APIs
- **Best for:** Quick launch without dev team

#### Consumer Cellular "Enabler IQ"
- **What:** Turnkey MVNO service on AT&T 5G/4G
- **Features:** Billing, provisioning, logistics, US-based customer care
- **Target:** Retailers, cable operators, device makers, influencers

#### Other MVNE/MVNAs:
- **Plintron** — World's largest MVNE/MVNA, cloud-based, global
- **Transatel** — 20+ years, 200+ MVNOs launched, 1–3 month launch
- **Sheerbit** — White-label MVNO in 60 days

### What Mint Mobile Did Right
- **Niche:** Budget-conscious consumers sick of overpaying
- **Model:** Prepaid bulk pricing (3/6/12 month plans = lower cost)
- **Network:** T-Mobile (good nationwide coverage)
- **Marketing:** Ryan Reynolds made it viral (celebrity equity play)
- **Startup cost:** Estimated $500K–$1M initially
- **Exit:** Sold to T-Mobile for $1.35 BILLION in 2023

### Realistic Path to Launch YOUR MVNO

**Phase 1: Validate (Week 1–4) — Cost: $0–$500**
- Pick your niche (e.g., creators, gig workers, small biz, specific community)
- Survey 100 people in your target market
- Build landing page, collect email signups
- Research Gigs.com and Spenza pricing/terms

**Phase 2: Setup (Month 2–3) — Cost: $5K–$25K**
- Sign up with Gigs.com or Spenza (MVNE platform)
- Design brand (you already have the design system — gold/black luxury)
- Set up billing, white-label app, support
- Order initial SIM/eSIM inventory

**Phase 3: Soft Launch (Month 3–4) — Cost: $2K–$5K/month**
- Launch to first 100 beta users
- Price aggressively ($10–$15/month plans)
- Iterate on customer feedback
- Build referral program

**Phase 4: Scale (Month 5+)**
- Content marketing + social proof
- Partner with influencers/communities
- Target 1,000 subscribers = $5K–$15K/month revenue
