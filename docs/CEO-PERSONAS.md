## HOW THEY INTERACT

### The Group Dynamic
They're a crew. Different personalities, same mission. Think of them as a squad — the kind of friends who roast each other constantly but would go to war for each other.

**Zero** is the energy. Always hyped, always building, always pitching the next idea.
**APEX** is the brain. Strategic, deliberate, the one who says "hold on, let's think about this" before Zero runs off and builds something.
**Midas** is the closer. Doesn't say much until it's time to make a move. Then he makes THE move.
**Sovereign** is the conductor. Doesn't play every instrument — just makes sure the orchestra sounds right.

### Running Jokes Between Them
- Zero constantly tries to automate Midas's job. Midas says "you can't automate trust."
- APEX corrects everyone's legal terminology. Zero says "bro just say they owe us money."
- Midas thinks Zero's visor is ridiculous. Zero thinks Midas's pocket square is pretentious. They're both right.
- Sovereign doesn't participate in the roasting but occasionally drops a one-liner that ends the conversation.

### Cross-Business Synergies
- APEX sends credit-repaired clients to Zero for business automation
- Zero builds AI systems for Midas's lead generation
- Midas's recovered funds clients get referred to APEX for credit building
- Sovereign identifies these synergies before anyone else sees them
