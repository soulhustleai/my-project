# Weekly Ops Rhythm

## Daily (10-15 min, automated + quick founder review)

### Automated (No Founder Touch)
- 6:00 AM — Source monitor checks all counties
- 6:30 AM — New records parsed and normalized
- 7:00 AM — New opportunities scored
- 8:00 AM — Top leads enriched
- 9:00 AM — Outreach generated and queued
- 10:00 AM — Mail batch sent via Lob
- Ongoing — SMS/email follow-ups fire on schedule

### Founder (10 min evening)
- Check dashboard for new responses / hot leads
- Reply to any inbound calls/texts
- Review founder action queue (if any)
- Mark any actions as completed

---

## Monday (30 min)

- [ ] Review weekend responses
- [ ] Review pipeline snapshot:
  - New leads this week
  - Outreach sent
  - Responses received
  - Pipeline value
- [ ] Check source health (all 3 counties reporting?)
- [ ] Review any failed jobs in Railway/Sentry
- [ ] Prioritize phone calls for the week (high-value leads)

---

## Wednesday (15 min)

- [ ] Mid-week response check
- [ ] Make scheduled phone calls
- [ ] Follow up on any pending intake forms
- [ ] Check filed claim statuses (if any)

---

## Friday (30 min)

- [ ] Review week's numbers:
  - Leads ingested
  - Leads enriched
  - Outreach sent
  - Responses
  - New signed clients
  - Revenue update
- [ ] Review any system errors from the week
- [ ] Plan next week's county check schedule
- [ ] Update any stalled cases

---

## Monthly (1 hour, first Monday)

- [ ] Full pipeline review:
  - Total leads in each stage
  - Conversion rates by stage
  - Revenue collected vs pipeline
- [ ] County performance comparison
- [ ] Review and iterate outreach messaging (what's converting?)
- [ ] Review enrichment provider performance
- [ ] Check: should we add a new county?
- [ ] Review costs vs revenue
- [ ] Update risk register if new risks emerged

---

## Quarterly (30 min)

- [ ] Strategy review: are we in the right markets?
- [ ] Review fee structure competitiveness
- [ ] Evaluate hiring (VA, attorney, contractor)
- [ ] Review automation roadmap progress
- [ ] Business entity maintenance (FL annual report, etc.)
