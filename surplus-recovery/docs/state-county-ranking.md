# State & County Ranking

## Methodology

States and counties ranked on: opportunity volume (25%), average surplus amount (15%), data accessibility (20%), filing simplicity (15%), statute of limitations (5%), competition density (10%), automation friendliness (10%).

Scale: 1-5 (5 = best)

## Best County Clusters (Recommended)

### Cluster 1: South Florida (PRIMARY — Launch Here)
- Broward County
- Palm Beach County
- Hillsborough County
- **Why:** High volume, good amounts, same state process, adjacent geography
- **Expand to:** Miami-Dade, Orange after validation

### Cluster 2: Ohio Triangle (SECONDARY — Month 2-3)
- Cuyahoga County
- Franklin County
- Hamilton County
- **Why:** Lower competition, simpler filing, no fee cap, online data

### Cluster 3: Arizona (SECONDARY — Month 2-3)
- Maricopa County (standalone)
- **Why:** Single county with enough volume to be viable alone

## States to AVOID or DELAY

| State | Reason |
|-------|--------|
