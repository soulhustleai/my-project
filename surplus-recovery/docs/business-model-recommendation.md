# Business Model Recommendation

## Launch Niche

**Tax sale overages + foreclosure surplus in Florida (Broward, Palm Beach, Hillsborough counties)**

### Why This Niche Wins
- Tax sale overages: simpler filing, faster payout, higher volume
- Foreclosure surplus: higher dollar amounts, added to pipeline as second product
- Florida: high volume, no fee cap, accessible data, strong property market
- Three-county cluster: shared state process, manageable scope, enough volume

### Why Alternatives Lose
- Single-process focus limits deal flow
- Non-Florida states have fee caps (TX, NC, GA) or complexity (NY, CA)
- Going national too early fragments attention and creates legal/process chaos

## Offer Structure

### The Offer
> "We found that [County] is holding $[amount] from the sale of your property at [address]. We help people recover these funds at no upfront cost. Our fee is [X]% of the recovered amount — you only pay if we successfully recover your money."

### What the Client Gets
1. Identification that funds exist and are claimable
2. Full claim preparation and document assembly
3. Filing with the county/court
4. Status tracking through to disbursement
5. No upfront cost, no risk

### What We Get
- Signed contingency agreement
- Required documents (ID, proof of ownership, notarized affidavit)
- Authorization to file on their behalf

## Fee Structure

| Surplus Amount | Recommended Fee | Rationale |
|---------------|----------------|-----------|
| $1,000 - $5,000 | 33% | Smaller amounts need higher % to justify work |
| $5,001 - $15,000 | 30% | Sweet spot for volume |
| $15,001 - $50,000 | 25% | Competitive rate, strong revenue per case |
| $50,001+ | 20-25% | Negotiate; client may push for lower % on large amounts |

### Fee Justification
- Industry standard: 25-40% contingency
- 30% is the market sweet spot — high enough to be profitable, low enough to close
- Below 25% for large amounts to remain competitive
- Above 33% risks claimant resistance and potential judicial scrutiny

### Fee Structure in Agreement
- Flat percentage of gross recovery
- Fee earned upon successful disbursement
- Client responsible for notarization costs only (typically $5-15)
- No hidden fees, no upfront costs

## Trust-Building Mechanism

Trust is the #1 conversion barrier. Claimants think it's a scam.

### Trust Stack (in order of deployment)
1. **Professional direct mail** — Printed letter on business letterhead with specific property details
2. **Verifiable business** — LLC registered, website live, phone number answers
3. **County verification language** — "You can verify these funds exist by contacting [County Clerk] at [phone] or visiting [URL]"
4. **No upfront payment** — "You pay nothing unless we recover your money"
5. **BBB/Google Business Profile** — Establish early for credibility
6. **Specific case details** — Including property address, case number, and approximate amount proves legitimacy
7. **Follow-up consistency** — Professional, persistent follow-up shows legitimacy (scammers don't follow up 7 times)
8. **Phone conversation** — For high-value cases, a real human conversation closes

## Intake Approach

### Digital-First with Manual Fallback

**Primary Flow (60% of clients):**
1. Client receives outreach → visits website or calls
2. Directed to intake form (Jotform/Tally)
3. Uploads ID, signs agreement (e-sign)
4. Notarization via remote online notarization (RON) service
5. System generates claim packet

**Manual Fallback (40% of clients):**
1. Client prefers phone/mail
2. Founder or VA walks them through intake on a call
3. Docs sent via mail with prepaid return envelope
4. Notarization at local notary (UPS Store, bank, mobile notary)
5. System generates claim packet upon doc receipt

### Required Documents (Typical)
- Government-issued photo ID
- Signed contingency fee agreement
- Notarized affidavit of claim
- Proof of identity/ownership connection (deed, court record)
- Power of Attorney or Assignment (if required by county)
- W-9 (for tax reporting on disbursement)

## Filing Approach

### County-Specific Templates
Each county gets its own filing template with:
- Required forms
- Required supporting documents
- Filing method (mail, online, in-person)
- Filing address/portal
- Expected timeline
- Follow-up procedure

### Filing Method Priority
1. **Online portal** (where available) — fastest
2. **Mail** — most common, reliable
3. **In-person** — last resort, only if required

## Staffing Model

### Phase 1: Solo Operator (Month 1-3)
- Edwin handles: high-value calls, final review of claim packets, any in-person filings
- System handles: lead generation, enrichment, outreach, follow-up, intake, doc generation
- Time commitment: 5-10 hrs/week

### Phase 2: Solo + VA (Month 3-6)
- Add VA for: data processing, mail preparation, follow-up calls, document chasing
- VA cost: $5-10/hr, 10-20 hrs/week = $200-$800/month
- Edwin focuses on: high-value client calls, complex cases, system improvement

### Phase 3: Scaled (Month 6+)
- Add contractor closer for phone sales (commission-based)
- Add paralegal/filing specialist for complex claims
- Add second VA for additional counties
- System continues to handle pipeline automation

## Speed-First Operating Motion

### Daily Rhythm
1. **Morning (automated):** System monitors sources, ingests new lists, enriches leads
2. **Midday (automated):** System queues outreach for new leads, sends follow-ups
3. **Evening (5-10 min founder):** Review dashboard, handle any escalations, make high-value calls
4. **Weekly (1-2 hrs founder):** Review pipeline, check filed claims, adjust strategy

### Key Speed Metrics
- Time from list publication to first outreach: **Target < 48 hours**
- Time from signed agreement to claim filed: **Target < 7 business days**
- Time from claim filed to first follow-up with county: **Target < 30 days**

## Solo vs Contractor vs Partner

| Option | Pros | Cons | Recommendation |
|--------|------|------|----------------|
| **Solo (with automation)** | Maximum profit, full control, lowest cost | Limited bandwidth, all on Edwin | **START HERE** |
| **Solo + VA** | Multiplies capacity, low cost | Training needed, quality control | **ADD AT MONTH 3** |
| **Attorney partner** | Handles complex cases, adds credibility | Cuts into margins, dependency | **PER-CASE BASIS** |
| **Business partner** | Shared workload | Shared profit, alignment risk | **NOT RECOMMENDED** at this stage |

---

## Bottom Line

Launch as a solo operator with heavy automation, targeting 3 Florida counties. Fee at 30% contingency. Trust built through professional presentation and verification language. Expand staffing only when pipeline exceeds solo capacity. This model gets to first revenue fastest with minimum overhead.
