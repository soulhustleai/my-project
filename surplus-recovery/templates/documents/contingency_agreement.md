### 1. SCOPE OF SERVICES

Recovery Agent agrees to assist Client in recovering surplus funds held by {{county}} County, {{state}}, related to the sale of real property located at:

**Property Address:** {{property_address}}
**Case/Reference Number:** {{case_number}}
**Estimated Surplus Amount:** ${{surplus_amount}}

Services include:
- Research and verification of surplus funds
- Preparation of all required claim documents
- Filing of claim with the appropriate governmental entity
- Follow-up and tracking of claim status
- Communication with Client regarding claim progress

### 2. CONTINGENCY FEE

Client agrees to pay Recovery Agent a fee of **{{fee_percentage}}%** of the gross amount successfully recovered ("Recovery Fee").

- The Recovery Fee is earned only upon successful recovery and disbursement of funds
- No fee is owed if recovery is unsuccessful
- Client is not required to pay any upfront fees or costs
- Client is responsible for any notarization costs required by the filing process (typically $5-$25)

### 3. PAYMENT

Upon successful recovery:
- Funds will be disbursed by the county/court to Recovery Agent or directly to Client (depending on county process)
- If funds are received by Recovery Agent, the Recovery Fee will be deducted and the remaining balance will be sent to Client within 10 business days
- If funds are received directly by Client, Client agrees to pay the Recovery Fee to Recovery Agent within 10 business days

### 4. CLIENT OBLIGATIONS

Client agrees to:
- Provide accurate identification and documentation as needed
- Sign required claim forms and affidavits
- Respond to requests for information in a timely manner
- Not engage another party to file a competing claim for the same funds during the term of this Agreement

### 5. TERM AND TERMINATION

- This Agreement remains in effect until the claim is resolved (approved, denied, or withdrawn)
- Client may terminate this Agreement at any time by providing written notice
- If Client terminates after Recovery Agent has filed the claim, and funds are subsequently recovered, the Recovery Fee remains owed
- Recovery Agent may withdraw from the claim at any time by providing written notice, in which case no fee is owed

### 6. NO LEGAL ADVICE

Recovery Agent is not an attorney and does not provide legal advice. This service involves document preparation and claim filing assistance only. Client is advised to consult an attorney for legal questions.

### 7. GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of {{state}}.
