# CHAPTER 3: BUILD YOUR FIRST DIGITAL PRODUCT IN ONE WEEKEND

This is the fastest path to revenue. Follow this exactly.

## Friday Night (2 hours): Research + Outline

**Step 1:** Go to TikTok Creative Center (ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter) and search your niche. What's trending? What are people asking about? What problems keep showing up?

**Step 2:** Use this Claude/ChatGPT prompt:

```
"I want to create a digital product (PDF guide/ebook) in
the [niche] space. Based on trending content and common
pain points, suggest:

1. 5 product ideas with titles that would sell
2. For each: the target buyer, the price point ($17-$97),
   and what problem it solves
3. The one that has the HIGHEST demand and LOWEST
   competition
4. A complete outline for that product (chapters/sections)
5. Key selling points for the sales page"
```

**Step 3:** Pick the winner. Write the outline. Done for the night.

## Saturday (4 hours): Write the Product

**The AI Writing Protocol:**

For each chapter, use this prompt:

```
"Write Chapter [X]: [Title] for my digital product
'[Product Name]'.

Target reader: [who they are]
Their problem: [what they're struggling with]
Tone: Conversational, funny, no-BS. Like a smart friend
who figured it out and is breaking it down for you.
Include: Actionable steps, specific tools/resources,
real examples, accountability nudges.
Length: 1,000-1,500 words.

Make it so clear a 5th grader could follow the steps.
Make it so good they feel dumb for not paying more."
```

Repeat for each chapter. Claude can write 1,000-1,500 words per prompt in about 30 seconds. A 10-chapter ebook = ~30 minutes of AI generation + 2-3 hours of your review, editing, and personal touch.

**Step 4:** Design in Canva.
- Use a Canva ebook template (search "ebook" in templates)
- Copy/paste your text in
- Add your brand colors and fonts
- Export as PDF

## Sunday (3 hours): Set Up the Sales Machine

**Step 1: Payhip (30 minutes)**
- Create account at payhip.com
- Upload PDF
- Set price
- Write description (use AI to generate it)
- Enable affiliate program (40-50% commission)

**Step 2: Stan Store (30 minutes)**
- Create bio link page
- Add product with Payhip checkout link
- Add to your TikTok/IG bio

**Step 3: ConvertKit (30 minutes)**
- Create email list
- Set up automation: new buyer → tag → welcome email
- AI writes the welcome email:

```
"Write a welcome email for someone who just purchased
'[Product Name]'. Thank them, set expectations for what
they'll learn, and tease the next product in the lineup.
Tone: warm, excited, like welcoming someone into a
community. Include a PS that asks them to reply with
their biggest takeaway (this boosts email deliverability)."
```

**Step 4: First 5 TikTok videos (1.5 hours)**

```
"Write 5 TikTok scripts (30-60 seconds each) to promote
my new digital product '[Product Name]'.

Each script should:
- Hook in the first 2 seconds
- Teach ONE thing from the product
- End with a CTA to 'link in bio'
- Be different from each other (different hooks, angles)

Script styles needed:
1. The 'I can't believe more people don't know this' reveal
2. The 'They don't want you to know this' authority play
3. The personal story / testimony format
4. The 'Stop doing X, start doing Y' correction
5. The trending sound / reaction format

Make them feel real, not salesy. Like I'm just sharing
something I discovered."
```

**Step 5: Schedule in Later.com (30 minutes)**
- Upload the 5 videos
- Schedule across TikTok + IG Reels + YouTube Shorts
- Set to post at peak times (Later suggests optimal times)

## Monday: You Have a Business

Your product is live. Your sales page is up. Your email captures buyers. Your content is scheduled. Your affiliate program means OTHER PEOPLE are promoting your product.

You built a business in one weekend. With AI doing 70% of the work.

# CHAPTER 4: THE CONTENT ENGINE — TRAFFIC ON AUTOPILOT

Content is the fuel. Without it, nothing moves. With it, everything is possible.

## The 30-Day Content Batch Process

**Once per month. 4 hours. 30 days of content.**

```
STEP 1: Research (30 minutes)
Claude prompt: "What are the top 10 trending topics in
[your niche] on TikTok right now? Include specific hooks
and angles that are getting high engagement."

STEP 2: Script Writing (30 minutes)
Claude prompt: "Write 21 TikTok/Reels scripts for my
[niche] account. Mix of all 5 content pillars:
1. Education (teach something valuable)
2. Authority (prove expertise)
3. Story (personal experience)
4. Trend (ride a current trend)
5. Promotion (subtle sell to products)
Make each 30-60 seconds. Include the hook, body, and CTA."

STEP 3: Record (2 hours)
Film all 21 videos in one session. Same setup, same
lighting, just swap scripts. OR use ElevenLabs for
faceless content over screen recordings.

STEP 4: Schedule (1 hour)
Upload all to Later.com. Schedule 3-5 posts/day across
TikTok + IG + YouTube Shorts.

RESULT: 30 days of daily content. Done in 4 hours.
Next content day: 30 days from now.
```
