## YOUR 5-MINUTE QUICK WIN (Do This Before You Read Another Word)

Go to annualcreditreport.com. Pull your free credit reports from all three bureaus — Experian, TransUnion, and Equifax. Yes, all three. Yes, right now. It's free. It takes 5 minutes. You can do this weekly now, not just once a year.

Don't analyze them yet. Don't panic when you see the numbers. Just pull them and save the PDFs to a folder called "Credit Ascension." By the time you finish Chapter 2 of this guide, you'll know EXACTLY what you're looking at and what to do about every single line.

Also — while you're at it, go to experian.com/boost. Sign up (free). Connect your bank account. It gives you credit for bills you're already paying — phone, Netflix, utilities. Average boost: 10-30 points. Your score literally just went up while you were sitting there.

**Two moves. Five minutes. Already winning.**
