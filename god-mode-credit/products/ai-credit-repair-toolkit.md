# CHAPTER 8: COMMON MISTAKES THAT KILL YOUR PROGRESS

## Mistake #1: Disputing Everything at Once
Don't dispute 15 items across all three bureaus simultaneously. The bureaus will flag your file as "frivolous" and auto-reject everything. One bureau at a time. 3-5 items per round. Patient and methodical.

## Mistake #2: Only Saying "Not Mine"
"This account is not mine" is the MOST COMMON dispute reason — and the easiest for bureaus to dismiss. Be specific: wrong balance, incorrect dates, improper status, mixed file, identity theft (with documentation). AI tools help you pick the RIGHT reason.

## Mistake #3: Disputing Online Instead of Mail
We covered this already but it's so important: certified mail gives you legal proof of delivery, more space to explain your dispute, and a paper trail. Online disputes go into a system designed to process them as fast as possible with minimal investigation.

## Mistake #4: Paying Collections Without a Delete Agreement
If you pay a collection without a pay-for-delete agreement IN WRITING, it changes from "unpaid collection" to "paid collection." Guess what? A paid collection still hurts your score. It just hurts slightly less. Get the deletion agreement FIRST. Then pay.

## Mistake #5: Not Building While You Clean
Don't wait until every negative is removed before building positive credit. Do both simultaneously. Open a credit builder account in Week 1. By the time your disputes are done in Month 3, you'll have 3 months of positive payment history already building.

## Mistake #6: Giving Up After "Verified"
"Verified" doesn't mean "game over." It means "dispute with a different strategy." Method of verification letter. Section 623 direct dispute. CFPB complaint. Different angle, different letter. The AI tools help you pivot quickly.
