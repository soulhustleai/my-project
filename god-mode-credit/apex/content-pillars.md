# APEX — Content Pillars
## TikTok / IG / YouTube Shorts Engine

> Every piece of short-form content Apex posts falls into one of five pillars. Each pillar has a hook template, a story structure, and a CTA. The goal is a repeatable machine — Claude generates scripts against this framework, Edwin films or ElevenLabs voices them, Later.com distributes.

---

## THE FIVE PILLARS

### PILLAR 1 — **THE LAW DROP** (Educational — FCRA/FDCPA/ECOA)
> One federal statute. One story. One action step.

**Posting cadence:** 3x per week

**Hook templates:**
- "You were told [common lie]. Fifteen U.S.C. [citation] says otherwise."
- "The bank does not want you to read this section of the code."
- "They bet on your silence. Silence is over."

**Structure (45-60s):**
1. Cold open — the lie most people believe (5s)
2. Reveal — the actual statute and what it says (15s)
3. Teach — what the statute gives you (15s)
4. Action — one concrete thing to do today (10s)
5. CTA — free lead magnet (5s)
6. Sign-off — "Ascend."

**Example:**
> "You were told disputes take months. Fifteen U.S.C. sixteen-eighty-one says thirty days. Section six-eleven of the FCRA. If the bureau cannot verify the item within thirty calendar days, it comes off. Not a suggestion. Federal law. Your action today: pull your report at annualcreditreport.com. Circle every item you do not recognize. Next video I show you the dispute letter template. The five laws are free — comment LAWS. Ascend."

### PILLAR 2 — **THE CROWNING STORY** (Transformation / Case Study)
> Real 300 → 750 stories, framed as coronations.

**Posting cadence:** 2x per week

**Hook templates:**
- "Three-twelve to seven-oh-one in ninety days. Here is how."
- "She was denied for a studio apartment. Six months later, she owns two rentals."
- "This is what ascension looks like."

**Structure (45-60s):**
1. Cold open — the rock bottom (10s)
2. The turning point — the moment they read The Five (10s)
3. The sequence — what they did, in order (20s)
4. The new life — where they are now (10s)
5. Sign-off — "Ascend."

**Rules:**
- Never use real customer names without consent
- Never guarantee replicable results — frame as "this student's path"
- Always cite at least one specific statute used
- Keep the transformation math plausible (90+ days)

### PILLAR 4 — **THE COVENANT MOMENT** (Spiritual / Mindset)
> Short, still, no music. Apex speaking to the denied, the ashamed, the underbanked.

**Posting cadence:** 1-2x per week

**Hook templates:**
- "Sit up. You are royalty."
- "Your credit score is not your worth."
- "The denial you got today is not a verdict on your life."

**Structure (30-45s):**
1. Cold open — a single heavy line (5s)
2. The teaching — 3 short verdicts (20s)
3. The invitation — the reader's role (10s)
4. Sign-off — "Ascend."

**Style notes:**
- Use Pose 2 (Prophet's Point) only
- Low sub-bass drone, NO melody
- Text on screen: none, or a single gold line of Cinzel font
- This pillar drives follows, not sales

**Example:**
> "Your credit score is not your worth. It is a language most people were never taught. Federal law exists specifically to protect you from the shame you have been carrying. You were never broken. You were never taught. Ascend."

### PILLAR 5 — **THE VERDICT** (Myth-Busting / Industry Call-Outs)
> Apex correcting bad credit advice. Sharp, short, clean.

**Posting cadence:** 1-2x per week

**Hook templates:**
- "They told you [myth]. The law says [verdict]."
- "This advice is costing you hundreds."
- "I am going to read this credit guru's advice out loud and then the actual statute."

**Structure (30-60s):**
1. Cold open — the bad advice screenshotted or quoted (10s)
2. The verdict — what the law actually says (20s)
3. The correction — what to do instead (15s)
4. Sign-off — "Ascend."

**Targets (what to bust):**
- "You can't dispute accurate debts" — false, you can dispute ANYTHING, the burden of proof is on them (FCRA 611)
- "Calling the collector is enough" — no, you need written validation under FDCPA 1692g
- "You need to pay off collections to remove them" — pay-for-delete is a negotiation; paying alone does nothing for your score
- "Credit repair companies are faster than DIY" — illegal under CROA unless they follow specific rules; you can do everything they can do, for free
- "Secured cards hurt your credit" — false, they report like any other card
- "Closing old cards helps your score" — false, it reduces average age of accounts

**Rules:**
- Never name-call specific gurus
- Always cite the statute, never just opinion
- End with the educational disclaimer on-screen

## BATCH PRODUCTION WORKFLOW

**Monday — Generate (30 min with Claude API)**
1. Open Claude in API workbench with Apex system prompt loaded
2. Run the "batch-21-scripts" prompt:
   > Generate 21 short-form content scripts this week: 3 Law Drops, 2 Armories, 2 Crowning Stories, 2 Verdicts, 1 Covenant Moment, 1 Crowning Story (flagship push). Each script must follow the structure in content-pillars.md. Each must end with "Ascend." Output as numbered Markdown with a file-safe slug for each.
3. Save output to `content/week-YYYY-WW.md`

**Tuesday — Voice (60 min with ElevenLabs)**
1. Strip Markdown from each script
2. Submit to ElevenLabs with APEX voice ID
3. Export 21 .mp3 files
4. Drop into Google Drive `/apex-content/week-YYYY-WW/`

**Wednesday — Assemble (90 min, video editor or Opus Clip)**
1. Import Apex static images (one per pose)
2. Layer voice track + static + b-roll + text overlay
3. Export 9:16 and 1:1 for each (42 exports)

**Thursday — Schedule (15 min, Later.com)**
1. Upload all 42 clips to Later
2. Drop into the 7-day grid
3. Pre-write captions (batch with Claude, same system prompt)

**Friday-Sunday — Monitor + respond to DMs (Apex ManyChat bot handles 80%)**

Total weekly time investment: **~3.5 hours** for 21 pieces of content distributed to 3 platforms.
