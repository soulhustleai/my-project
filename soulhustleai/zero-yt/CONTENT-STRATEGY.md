# Zero YT — Content Strategy
## Pillars, cadence, and hook formulas that compound

---

## The 5 Content Pillars

Every script must fit one of these. If it doesn't, don't shoot it.

### Pillar 1 — "My Staff Don't Exist" (IDENTITY)
Zero as a character. Origin stories, behind-the-scenes, how an AI runs a company.
**Examples:**
- "I'm an AI. Here's how I became a CEO."
- "Why my boss built me instead of hiring humans."
- "A day in the life of Zero — from 6am calls to midnight quotes."

**Purpose:** Build the character so people care about the brand, not the product.

### Pillar 2 — Case Studies (PROOF)
Real clients, real numbers, real receipts. Eli is the flagship. Adam is #2.
**Examples:**
- "This junk removal guy made $11,400 in 30 days with 1 AI automation."
- "How a Florida health insurance advisor 10x'd his leads in 2 weeks."
- "I built a $797/mo system for an appliance repair shop. Here's what happened."

**Purpose:** Stack social proof. Every case study is a sales asset forever.

### Pillar 3 — BOSSMoves Framework (EDUCATION)
The 4 pillars: Lead Gen → Conversion → Fulfillment → Ascension.
**Examples:**
- "The 4-step framework every service business needs (BOSSMoves)."
- "Stop chasing leads. Build a pipe that fills itself."
- "Pillar 4 is how Eli went from $400/mo to $1,497/mo with me."

**Purpose:** Position Zero as the authority. Every framework video is a credibility deposit.

### Pillar 4 — The 5 Automations (UTILITY)
Speed to Lead, Doc Processing, Follow-up, DB Reactivation, Internal Reporting.
**Examples:**
- "Speed to Lead: why 78% of deals go to the first responder."
- "Database Reactivation: your dead list is $4,500/mo of free money."
- "Follow-up sequences: 80% of sales happen after the 5th contact."

**Purpose:** Utility videos are the ones that get shared and saved.

### Pillar 5 — "They Profit Off Your Ignorance" (DIVINE ANGLE)
Spiritual/sovereignty angle. Calls out gatekeepers, shares the "keys."
**Examples:**
- "They charge $10K for what I'll show you for free."
- "The reason your business is broken isn't you. It's the system."
- "AI isn't stealing jobs. It's giving them back."

**Purpose:** Creates tribe. This is where loyal followers come from.

## Cadence

### Week 1 launch
- **3 long-form** (origin, BOSSMoves, Eli case) — seeds the channel
- **9 shorts** (3 per long-form, Opus Clip cut)

### Steady state (after week 1)
- **2 long-form per week** (Tue + Fri)
- **5 shorts per week** (daily M-F)
- **1 community post per week** (poll, question, update)

### Batch day = Sunday
All scripts written, all audio generated, all videos edited, all thumbnails made on Sunday. Upload schedule handled by Later.com.

## Hook Formulas (first 3 seconds are everything)

### "Receipts" hook
> "I just watched a junk removal guy pull $11,400 out of missed calls.
> Let me show you exactly how."

### "Pattern break" hook
> "If your staff answered the phone the way my AI does — you'd fire them by lunch."

### "Contrarian" hook
> "Everyone tells you to hire more reps. I'm gonna tell you to fire all of them."

### "Story loop" hook
> "Last month, Eli told me he was done with junk removal.
> This morning he texted me asking if I can build him 3 more automations.
> Here's what changed."

### "Number hook" hook
> "There are exactly 5 automations that print money in service businesses.
> I'm going to walk you through all 5 in the next 7 minutes."

### "Authority flex" hook
> "I'm an AI CEO. I answer 200+ calls a day and haven't taken a sick day
> since 2026. Here's my playbook."

## Long-Form Structure (5-9 min videos)

```
0:00–0:05  HOOK (one of the formulas above)
0:05–0:15  PROMISE + stakes (what they'll learn + why it matters)
0:15–0:30  PATTERN INTERRUPT (b-roll + 1 wild stat)
0:30–1:00  INTRO (who Zero is — only for first-time viewers)
1:00–6:00  MEAT (3-5 points, each with a story/example/number)
6:00–7:30  CASE STUDY (Eli, Adam, or anonymized prospect)
7:30–8:15  CTA #1 (soft) — "if you want this built for you, link in description"
8:15–9:00  RECAP + CTA #2 (strong) — "call me at (929) 236-1567"
```

---

## Shorts Structure (15-45 sec)

```
0:00–0:02  HOOK
0:02–0:25  PUNCHLINE or ONE KILLER TIP
0:25–0:30  CTA — "call Zero at 929-236-1567" or "link in bio"
```

Shorts goal: **one idea, one punchline, one CTA.** Never two.

## Thumbnails

### Long-form template
- Left 60%: Zero's face (gold visor, black hoodie)
- Right 40%: 2-3 words in Cinzel Bold, gold on black, with drop shadow
- Red/gold accent element (arrow, circle, or dollar sign)

### Shorts
- No thumbnail needed (auto-selects from first frame — ensure first frame is text-heavy)

## Cross-Platform Distribution

Every long-form video gets repurposed into:
- 3 YouTube Shorts (Opus Clip)
- 3 TikToks (same clips, different captions)
- 3 IG Reels
- 1 Twitter thread (key takeaways)
- 1 LinkedIn post (professional angle)
- 1 newsletter section (educational)

**Total reach per video: ~12 distribution points.**
