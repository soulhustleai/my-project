# Zero's Quickfire YT Intro
## 5-second cold open that runs at the front of every video + every short

> **Goal:** Instant brand recognition in under 5 seconds. Comedy + swagger + education primer. Uses Orlando voice + Zero avatar.
> **Runtime:** 4-6 seconds (never longer)
> **Loop:** same intro every video — familiarity compounds
> **Audio file to render:** `/zero-yt/assets/intro-v1.wav` (render from ElevenLabs)

---

## VERSION 1 — THE DAILY (primary)

### Audio script (Orlando voice)
> **"Yo.** You in the Zero zone. *(beat)* Staff don't exist, but the **money** does. *(beat)* Roll it."

### Timing breakdown
| t | Line | Visual | Notes |
|---|------|--------|-------|
| 0.0s | "Yo." | Black screen → gold compass core FLASH ON | Hard cut in |
| 0.4s | "You in the" | Zero avatar slides in from left | Gold particle trail |
| 1.0s | "Zero zone." | Full Zero portrait, visor glint | Quick zoom |
| 1.6s | *(beat)* | Hold on Zero | Subtle ambient hum |
| 1.9s | "Staff don't exist," | Small gold text overlay bottom: "MY STAFF DON'T EXIST" | Cinzel, gold `#F2C95C` |
| 2.8s | "but the **MONEY** does." | Text morphs to stack of $$$ icons or a revenue counter spinning up | Teal `#14B8A6` accent on "MONEY" |
| 3.9s | *(beat)* | Zero smirks, tilts head | Half second of silence = confidence |
| 4.2s | "Roll it." | Cut to the video content | Gold slash transition |
| 5.0s | — | [main video starts] | — |

### Sound design
- **0.0s:** bass thump + synth stab (signature Zero "ID" sound, ~C minor, punchy)
- **1.0s:** gold chime (the compass hit — signature)
- **2.8s:** sub bass drop on "MONEY"
- **4.2s:** vinyl scratch + gold sparkle → transitions into main video
