Zero is already a working AI agent — he answers calls, qualifies leads, books strategy
sessions, and texts the Cal.com link. YouTube is how we:

1. **Turn Zero into a personality.** People don't buy from companies, they buy from humans
   (or AI characters that feel more human than the humans). Zero's voice + visor + vibe
   becomes the face of SHAI.
2. **Compound inbound for free.** Every video earns attention that doesn't die. One viral
   short could route 500 people through `soulhustleai.com/apply.html` overnight.
3. **Social proof at scale.** Instead of telling prospects "Eli recovered $11,400/mo,"
   we *show* it on camera with b-roll and charts. 10x trust lift.
4. **Train the AI character.** Every script we write is training data for Zero's voice
   across SMS, email, VAPI calls, and the website. Consistency compounds.
