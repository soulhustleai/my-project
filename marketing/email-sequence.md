## EMAIL 4 — The 3 Types of Debt
**Subject:** Not all debt on your report is the same. Here's how to handle each type.
**Send:** Day 3

---

[First Name],

There are three types of negative items on credit reports. Each requires a different strategy.

**Type 1: Inaccurate Information**
Something that's just wrong. Wrong balance, wrong date, account that isn't yours.
→ Strategy: FCRA dispute. Certified mail to the bureau. 30 days. If unverifiable, it must be removed.

**Type 2: Legitimate Debt (That You Owe)**
A real collection, a real late payment.
→ Strategy: Options here. You can try FCRA dispute even on real items — if they can't verify the specific data, it still goes. OR you can negotiate a pay-for-delete (pay the debt in exchange for full removal). OR you can let it age off (most negative items expire at 7 years).

**Type 3: Expired Items (Past the 7-Year Mark)**
Anything past 7 years from the date of first delinquency (or 10 years for Chapter 7 bankruptcy) is legally required to be removed.
→ Strategy: Write to the bureau citing FCRA § 1681c(a). It must come off.

Knowing which type you're dealing with determines your move.
