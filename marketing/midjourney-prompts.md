## PRO TIPS FOR MIDJOURNEY

- Run each prompt at least 4 times to get multiple variations
- Use **V1/V2/V3/V4** to vary your favorites
- Use **U1/U2/U3/U4** to upscale your best picks
- If text renders wrong, re-run — MJ v6.1 text is probabilistic
- Add `--chaos 20` for more variation between the 4 results
- Add `--quality 2` for maximum detail (uses 2x GPU time)
- The Flagship (Cover 6) — run 8+ times, this is your hero product
