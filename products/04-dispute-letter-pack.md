## LETTER 1 — Standard FCRA Dispute (Inaccurate Information)

**Use when:** Any information on your report is incorrect (wrong balance, wrong date, wrong account number, not yours)

---
[Your Name]
[Address]
[City, State, ZIP]
[Phone]
[Date]

[Bureau Name] Dispute Department
[Bureau Address]

**Re: FORMAL DISPUTE — Inaccurate Credit Information**
**SSN:** [Last 4 digits only]

Dear [Bureau Name] Dispute Department:

I am writing to formally dispute inaccurate information appearing on my credit report. Pursuant to my rights under the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA), 15 U.S.C. § 1681i, I request that the following information be investigated and corrected or removed:

**Account in Dispute:**
- Creditor/Collector Name: [Name]
- Account Number: [Full or partial number]
- Nature of Inaccuracy: [Describe specifically]

The information reported is inaccurate because [specific reason]. I have enclosed [documentation supporting your dispute if available].

Under the FCRA, you are required to conduct a reasonable investigation of this dispute within 30 days of receipt of this letter. If the information cannot be verified as accurate, it must be promptly deleted from my report.

Please provide me with written notification of the results of your investigation.

Sincerely,
[Signature]
[Printed Name]

**Enclosures:** Copy of credit report with disputed item circled, [any supporting documents]

## LETTER 2 — Unverifiable Debt Dispute

**Use when:** You want the bureau to verify the debt — because if they can't verify it, they must remove it

---
[Your Name] | [Address] | [Date]

[Bureau Name] Dispute Department

**Re: REQUEST FOR VERIFICATION — Unverifiable Account**

Dear Dispute Department:

I am disputing the following account pursuant to FCRA § 1681i, as I have reason to believe it may not be verifiable:

**Account:** [Creditor Name] | Account #[Number] | Balance: $[Amount]

Under the FCRA, if you cannot verify the accuracy of this information with the furnishing party, you are required to delete it from my credit file. I am requesting that you conduct a thorough investigation and provide me with:

1. The name and contact information of the person at the furnishing company who verified this information
2. The method used to verify this information
3. A complete copy of all documentation used in your investigation

If you are unable to fully verify this information within 30 days, I demand it be immediately deleted.

[Signature + Printed Name]

Reporting a single debt multiple times artificially inflates negative information and violates the accuracy standards of FCRA § 1681e(b). I request all duplicate entries be removed, retaining only the original account (or removing all if the debt is unverifiable).

## LETTER 8 — Goodwill Deletion Request (to Creditor Directly)

**Use when:** You have a good payment history with a creditor but one late payment — appeal to their goodwill

*Note: Send to original CREDITOR, not the bureau*

---
[Your Name] | [Address] | [Date]

[Creditor Name] Customer Relations Department
[Creditor Address]

**Re: Goodwill Deletion Request — Account #[Number]**

Dear Customer Relations Team,

I have been a customer of [Creditor Name] since [year] and have always valued our relationship. I am writing to respectfully request a goodwill deletion of the late payment notation recorded on [date] for the above account.

At the time of this late payment, I was experiencing [brief, genuine hardship — medical issue, job loss, family emergency, etc.]. Since then, I have maintained a perfect payment history and have [X payments] on time consecutively.

I understand this was a blemish on my account. I am not disputing the payment occurred late — I am asking, as a loyal customer, if you would consider removing this notation as a gesture of goodwill. This would significantly help my credit profile and I would deeply appreciate it.

Thank you for your consideration.

Sincerely,
[Signature + Printed Name]

## LETTERS 11–15 — Rapid-Fire Templates

### Letter 11 — Inquiry Dispute
*For hard inquiries you didn't authorize*
"I am disputing an unauthorized hard inquiry from [Company] on [date]. I did not authorize this inquiry. Under FCRA § 1681b, a permissible purpose is required for any credit inquiry. Please remove this inquiry immediately or provide documentation of my written authorization."

### Letter 12 — Medical Debt Dispute
*New CFPB rules — medical debt under $500 cannot be reported as of 2025*
"The medical debt reported by [Collector] in the amount of $[amount] falls under new CFPB medical debt reporting rules effective 2025. Medical debts under $500 are no longer reportable. I request immediate removal."

### Letter 13 — Debt Settlement (Settled / Not Full Pay) Status Update
"Account [X] shows 'settled for less than full amount.' While accurate, I request this be updated to 'paid/closed' per my agreement with [creditor] dated [date]. Enclosed is documentation of our settlement agreement."

### Letter 14 — Mixed File Dispute
*When someone else's info is on your report*
"My credit file contains information belonging to another person. [Account/personal info] does not belong to me — I believe this is a mixed file error. I request a thorough file separation investigation and removal of all information not belonging to me."

### Letter 15 — Final Demand Before Legal Action
*After 30-day investigation with no resolution*
"Your office has failed to properly investigate my dispute submitted on [date]. This is my final demand for resolution before I pursue civil litigation under FCRA § 1681n for willful noncompliance and § 1681o for negligent noncompliance. I am prepared to seek actual damages, statutory damages up to $1,000, punitive damages, and attorney fees."
